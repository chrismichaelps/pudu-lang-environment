# Loading

[[src/PuduLangEnvironment/Options]] says what to read, [[src/PuduLangEnvironment/Discovery]] finds
it, [[src/PuduLangEnvironment/Source]] reads it, [[src/PuduLangEnvironment/Loader]] parses and
merges it through [[src/PuduLangEnvironment/Domain/Layers]], and
[[src/PuduLangEnvironment/Variables]] answers it. See [[domain/Layer]], [[domain/Discovery]], and
[[domain/Variables]].
